Meet your Ottawa baker

Farah found a new home through baking.

After coming to Canada alone as a new immigrant, Farah discovered in baking a passion—and a beautiful way to belong more and more to her new home in Ottawa.

Her forte is unforgettable flavour: exquisite, fresh and made with premium ingredients. Every order is approached with genuine flexibility, so dietary needs, preferences and special ideas feel welcome.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

How much does a custom cake cost in Ottawa?

Custom cakes at Madame La Joie start at $39.99. Final pricing depends on size, design and flavours — request a free quote through our order form.

Do you offer cake pickup or delivery in Ottawa?

Yes! We offer both pickup and delivery across Ottawa and Gatineau. Choose your preference when placing an order.

Can you accommodate allergies and dietary needs?

Absolutely. Flexibility is one of Farah's specialties — tell us about any allergies or dietary preferences and we will tailor your order.

How far in advance should I order a custom cake?

We recommend ordering as early as possible, ideally one to two weeks ahead. Contact us for last-minute requests — we will do our best to help.

Do you cater tea parties in Ottawa?

Yes, Farah offers a special tea party setup service upon request, complete with handcrafted desserts and elegant table styling. Reach out for details.
