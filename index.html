Meet the baker

Farah found a new home through baking.

After coming to Canada alone as a new immigrant, Farah discovered in baking a passion—and a beautiful way to belong more and more to her new home.

Her forte is unforgettable flavour: exquisite, fresh and made with premium ingredients. Every order is approached with genuine flexibility, so dietary needs, preferences and special ideas feel welcome.
